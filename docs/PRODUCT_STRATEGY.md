# Product and design contract

A local salon website for women, men and families in Andheri East. The primary journey is explore services → choose a service → prepare a preferred appointment enquiry → send through WhatsApp → salon confirms. No payment, account, personal-data persistence or invented availability.

## Visual direction

Plum ink #33252e; pale orchid #e0d3ea; porcelain #faf8f6; muted rose #bba1a8; text #352d32; muted text #70646c. Cormorant Garamond for editorial display type; Manrope for interface and body. Large asymmetrical portrait hero, a narrow salon-category ribbon, image-led service collection, lilac family service chooser, dark treatment feature, staggered inspiration gallery, verified reviews, and a practical contact section. Rounded buttons pair with softly curved portrait masks.

The initial concept was revised from a generic gold/cream spa treatment to an aubergine/lilac fashion direction. The memorable gesture is an oversized ampersand behind the portrait composition, connecting men, women and family. Avoid unnecessary metrics, numbering, gradients and boxed content.

Motion: staged hero entrance, desktop-only photographic parallax, drawn hair-strand curves and scissors, scroll-linked facial-care graphic, image masks and category transitions. Native scrolling, limited transform/opacity work, reduced-motion fallback, and no autoplay review carousel.

## Acceptance

All requested sections; working service filters; service-aware appointment enquiry with validation and explicit unconfirmed state; WhatsApp prefilled text when a verified number is configured; accessible gallery dialog with keyboard arrows; responsive navigation; map directions; local optimized images and fonts; production build; desktop/tablet/mobile browser review; keyboard and reduced-motion checks; no false reviews, prices or result promises.
